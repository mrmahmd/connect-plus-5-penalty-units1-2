How to play

Answer one English challenge, lock your answer, then choose a target in the goal. A correct answer scores a goal. An incorrect answer is saved; read the correction and try the next question. Earn 100 XP per goal, plus 25 XP for each step in a streak (bonus capped at 100 XP). There is no answer time limit.

Quick Match covers every selected lesson once. Cup Run gives two shots per lesson. Full Training uses the complete matching bank. Skill focus can narrow lesson coverage. Your final report shows exactly what you played.

Printed textbook page numbers are shown. Literature Corner (Lesson 6 in each unit) is excluded. The separate story lessons remain included. This game practises recognition, grammar and comprehension; speaking and extended writing remain classroom activities.
